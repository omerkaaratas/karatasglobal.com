What We Do

Clear requirements. Suitable options. Commercially sensible decisions.

Karatas Global helps businesses and buyers identify, source and coordinate suitable commercial vehicles, trailers, equipment and project-related products across Ireland and Europe.

We work directly with clients to understand their commercial requirements, identify appropriate options, and coordinate the full sourcing process — from initial enquiry through to delivery. Transport, documentation and customs clearance are coordinated through appropriate third-party providers where required.

We focus on specification, condition, price, availability and delivery before presenting an option.
