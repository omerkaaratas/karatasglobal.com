WEBSITE PRIVACY

How we handle enquiry information

This notice is proposed for founder approval before activating online form submissions. This notice concerns the contact and enquiry pathways on karatasglobal.com, and does not imply registration or incorporation.

Who to contact

For privacy questions relating to Karatas Global website enquiries, contact Omer Karatas, the founder and website contact, at omer@karatasglobal.com, Dublin, Ireland. Formal legal/trading identity, if different, must be confirmed before publication.

Information you provide

For buyer enquiries we ask for name, company, business email, buying market and sourcing requirement, with optional quantity and timing. For supplier or partnership enquiries we ask for name, company, business email, country, sector/products and reason for enquiry, with optional export experience and target market.

Please do not include confidential drawings, trade secrets, payment information, identity documents or sensitive personal data in an initial form.

Purpose and lawful basis

We use these details to understand and respond to your business enquiry, evaluate whether further discussions are appropriate, and document any mutually agreed next steps. This is generally necessary to take steps at your request before a potential business engagement or for our legitimate interest in responding to genuine commercial enquiries. We do not automatically subscribe you to marketing.

How submission information is handled

Once direct forms are activated, Formspark will receive form submissions, check them for spam and make them available through the provider's service and configured notification emails. This is an external processing service, not a Karatas Global-operated server. The destination notification mailbox is intended to be omer@karatasglobal.com and must be verified before activation. Technical request metadata, such as time and IP address, may also be processed by the form provider. See Formspark's legal information.

Our business email service processes emails sent to the published business address. If you instead choose your own email app or telephone, those services handle the contact under their own terms. No advertising analytics or cookies are intentionally added by these forms.

Retention and your choices

We intend to keep unconverted enquiries only for the period reasonably necessary to assess and follow up, ordinarily no longer than six months unless there is an active discussion or a lawful reason for longer retention. Business records required by legal or accounting obligations may be kept for the applicable statutory period. Founder must approve and implement this deletion practice before accepting live submissions.

You can request access, correction or deletion of your personal data, or object to relevant processing, by emailing omer@karatasglobal.com. You may also contact Ireland's Data Protection Commission.

Draft prepared: 23 October 2026. Publication and final business/controller details require founder verification.
